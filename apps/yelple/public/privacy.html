Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Overview

Yelple is designed to collect minimal personal data. This policy explains what may be collected and how it is used.

2. Data Stored in Your Browser

Yelple stores gameplay streak data in your browser local storage so your streak can persist on the same device/browser. This data stays on your device unless your browser syncs it.

3. Server Data

Yelple may store gameplay events such as challenge date, guessed rating, and correctness for analytics and service quality. Standard server logs (such as IP address, user-agent, and timestamps) may be collected automatically by hosting infrastructure.

4. Third-Party Providers

Yelple may request business content from third-party providers, including Yelp APIs. Your interactions with those providers are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Yelple does not currently rely on complex tracking cookies for gameplay. If advertising or analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

6. Data Retention

Provider-sourced content may be cached temporarily and purged according to provider requirements. Internal gameplay analytics may be retained longer for product improvement.

7. Your Choices

You can clear local storage from your browser settings at any time to reset streak data. You can stop using Yelple at any time.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be fully secure.

9. Children

Yelple is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will review and remove it as appropriate.

10. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated periodically. For privacy requests or questions, contact: privacy@yelple.example (replace with your real email).
